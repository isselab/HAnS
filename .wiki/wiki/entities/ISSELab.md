# ISSELab

Research group behind HAnS. Chair of Software Engineering at Ruhr University Bochum (RUB).

- GitHub: https://github.com/isselab
- Website: https://se.rub.de/research-group/

## Role in HAnS

Primary maintainer and publisher. HAnS originated from academic research on feature-oriented software development.

## Key Researchers (HAnS)

- Johan Martinson (johmara) — maintainer, co-author on all publications
- Herman Jansson (janssonherman) — co-author, reviewer
- Thorsten Berger — supervisor/PI on multiple publications
- Kevin Hermann (HerrKermet) — reviewer
